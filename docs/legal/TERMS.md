## The game

Ark Pals is a Bible adventure game for children ages 4 to 7, played on a tablet, phone or computer with a
grown-up's care. We keep adding to it and improving it, so things will change. During our free church beta,
some things may also break, and we may change or end the beta at any time. You can download your family's
data whenever you like (Parent Corner, "Download everything").

## Your family's account

- Only grown-ups (18 or older) sign in. A parent starts a family and is responsible for who they invite, and
  for the children who play on the family's devices.
- One family plan covers your whole family: up to 4 players, and the grown-ups you invite.
- Sign-in links work once, for the person they're sent to. Don't share them, and sign out devices you no
  longer use (Parent Corner, "Signed-in devices").
- You can leave a family, or delete your family's account, at any time (Parent Corner, "Data and privacy").

- **The free week.** A new family's first paid plan starts with 7 days free. You can cancel during that week
  and pay nothing. A family gets the free week once.
- **It renews until you cancel.** A monthly plan renews each month, and a yearly plan each year, on the card
  you gave, until you cancel. Cancel any time on the billing page (Parent Corner, "Your family's plan",
  "Billing page"): the plan stays on until the end of the time you've paid for, and isn't charged again.
- **Grace codes.** A Grace code from a church or ministry gives the Family plan free for 6 or 12 months. It
  asks for no card, and when the free months end, the plan simply ends: nothing is ever charged. If you'd
  like to keep going, you can add a card on the billing page, or choose a plan. Each code works once, and
  codes can't be sold or exchanged for money.
- **Sponsor a Family** ($9.99 a month) is a gift, not a plan for your own family: each month it pays for one
  Grace code that we give to a family who needs it through children's ministries. Cancel it any time on the
  billing page.

  acts as the seller of record for the payment: it works out and collects the sales tax, sends your

- **Refunds.** If something's wrong with a charge, write to us (below) and we'll make it right. Link may also

- **Price changes.** If we change a plan's price, we'll tell you in the game and by email at least 30 days

- **Families on no plan.** Families who haven't chosen a plan, or whose plan has ended, may find the islands
