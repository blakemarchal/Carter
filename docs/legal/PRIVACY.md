# Ark Pals Privacy Policy

> **DRAFT for legal review** (docs/BUSINESS-PLAN.md §5). Written to match exactly what the game and server
> do as of October 2026. Items in [brackets] are still to confirm. Not yet published.

Ark Pals is a Bible adventure game for children ages 4 to 7, made by Marchal Technologies LLC
("we"). This policy says what we keep, why, who else sees it, and how a family can see or delete all of it.

**In short:** grown-ups sign in, not children. We keep only what the game needs to work for your family.
We never show ads, never sell or rent information, use no third-party tracking or analytics, and nothing
is shared between families. **No child's name ever leaves our server.** A parent can download or delete
everything at any time in the Parent Corner.

## Who uses Ark Pals

Accounts belong to families, and only grown-ups sign in. A parent starts a family and invites other
grown-ups (a grandparent, the other parent) with a one-time link. Children play on a device a grown-up has
signed in; they never type an email address or create an account, and there's no chat or any way to
contact other people.

## What we keep, and why

**On your device** (in the app's own storage, which stays on the device):
- each player's first name or nickname, an emoji, how their character looks, and optionally their birthday,
  so the game can greet them and hold their birthday party;
- the family names a parent adds for the stories (what the children call their grown-ups, brothers,
  sisters and pets);
- each player's progress, Pals, stickers and settings;
- copies of the game itself, narration clips, the house narrator's recordings and songs, so it works offline.

**On our server**, for your family only:
- your family's name, and each grown-up's name, role (parent or grown-up), chosen avatar, and email
  address if they add one (to be sent a sign-in link);
- the devices signed in to your family: a name like "iPad", and when each was first and last used;
- backups of your players' progress (copies of what's on each device, so a lost or reset device doesn't
  lose it): the newest 60 are kept;
- voice recordings a grown-up makes in Family voices (story pages, and their children's names, so the
  game can say a name in their own voice), and songs a grown-up adds, for your family's devices to play.

**Names families use**, counted: so our narrator can record the children's names families use that aren't
in her name library yet, we count how many families use each child's first name (read from the players and
brothers and sisters in each family's newest progress backup). We (the site owner) see a name only when
three or more families use it, and then only the name and how many families, never which families. Nothing
is kept: the count is made when we look.

**Play totals**, only if a parent turns them on (Parent Corner, "Help make Ark Pals better"; off until
then, and set on each device separately). They help us see where children stop, replay or find things
hard, so we can make the game better. Exactly this is kept:
- for each day (the date only, never the time), how many times each island, each visit of an island, and
  each kind of activity in a visit (a story, a quiz, a maze…) was **started**, **finished** and
  **replayed** (started again by a player who had already finished that island), and how many questions
  in each activity were **answered wrong the first time**;
- for each day, in practice rounds: how many times each kind of learning exercise (building a word or a
  sentence, the number line, the clock, coins, measuring) and the questions of each skill (reading,
  numbers) were **started**, **finished** and **got right the first time** (with no wrong tries and no
  help), at each level (1 to 10);
- for each day, how many times the first-time treasure hunt (which finds where to start reading and
  numbers) **ended at each level**, for each skill (one count per skill when the hunt is finished);
- nothing else: no names, no players or which player did what, no devices, no answers, no free text and
  no times of day. Counts from all the devices that share are added together into one set of totals for
  your family.

They're kept on our server with the rest of your family's data, for 400 days (about 13 months), after
which each day's counts are deleted. They're in your family's download, deleted when the family is
deleted, and a parent can delete them at any time ("Delete our play totals"). Turning sharing off on a
device stops it counting and drops anything it hasn't sent yet. We (the site owner) look only at sums
across every family that shares, never at one family's totals, and no sums are shown at all until at
least five families share, so one family's play can't be picked out. They're never shared with anyone
else or used for advertising.

**Sign-in:** one cookie, set by us, keeps a signed-in device signed in. It isn't used for anything else.

We don't collect location, contacts, photos or anything else from the device, and the game makes no
requests to anyone else's servers [confirm: fonts and all other files are served by us].

## Who else handles it

We use a few service providers, only to run the game:
- **DigitalOcean** hosts our server in the United States, where everything above is stored.
- **xAI** made some of the narration (the "Ara" voice) ahead of time, while we built the game: the
  game's own fixed lines, and nothing else. **Nothing from your family is ever sent to xAI, and no child's
  name ever leaves our server.** The narrator is our own family's recorded voice (we call it the house
  narrator). Where a line has a child's name in it, the game puts it together from recordings: your own
  recording of the name (Family voices), or our narrator's recording of it from her library of common
  children's names. If neither has the name, that line is read by the device's own voice, on the device.
- **Resend** sends our emails (sign-in links and invitations) when a grown-up asks for one; it receives
  that email address and the email.
- **ImprovMX** forwards email sent to hello@spiritflow.church to us.
- **Stripe** handles payments when a family chooses a plan. It receives the email of the parent who starts
  the plan, what they enter on Stripe's own checkout page (card and billing address), and our id for the
  family, and nothing about the children. We never see full card numbers. We keep the family's Stripe
  customer id, plan, status and paid-through date; they're in the family's download, and deleting the
  family deletes its Stripe customer too, which ends the plan (Stripe keeps its own record of past payments,
  as the law requires). [Billing is in Stripe's test mode until launch.]

We don't sell, rent or trade information, and we share it with no one else, except if required by law.

## Children's privacy

Ark Pals is made for children, so we follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We
collect the least we can: a child's first name or nickname, how their character looks, an optional
birthday, their progress, and any recordings their grown-ups choose to make. All of it is entered by a
parent or played on a device a parent signed in. [Consent: at paid launch, a parent's card payment;
for free families, a confirmation email and a consent checkbox. To confirm with counsel.]

A parent can review everything we keep about their family, download it, or delete it (below), and can
stop us collecting more at any time by deleting the family.

## Seeing, downloading and deleting your family's data

In the Parent Corner, under "Your family's data", a parent can:
- see what's kept, and how much room is left for recordings and songs;
- **download everything** as one file: your grown-ups and devices, every progress backup, every recording
  and every song, and your play totals (if you share them);
- **delete the family's account**, which removes every grown-up's sign-in, every device, and all your
  backups, recordings, songs and play totals from our server right away. (What's on each device stays on that device
  until the app is removed.)

Grown-ups can also leave a family, and parents can remove grown-ups or sign devices out. For anything else,
write to hello@spiritflow.church.

## How long we keep it

- Your family's information stays until you delete it or the family is deleted.
- Progress backups: the newest 60 for each family.
- Play totals (only if a parent turned them on): 400 days, then each day's counts are deleted.
- Invitation and sign-in links stop working after 30 minutes to 30 days, depending on the kind, and once used.
- [Copies of the server made before an update are kept for up to 30 days.]
- [At launch: a family's data is deleted 90 days after a subscription ends.]

## Keeping it safe

Everything travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Sign-in links work once and are stored only in
scrambled form; sign-in cookies can't be read by scripts. Each family's files are kept apart, and only we
can reach the server. See our data-security plan (docs/legal/DATA-SECURITY.md) [publish a summary].
