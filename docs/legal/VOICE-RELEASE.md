# Ark Pals Voice Release

> **DRAFT for legal review** (docs/BUSINESS-PLAN.md §2 and §5). One copy is signed for each voice: Mom,
> Dad, and our child (signed by both parents for her). Names go in when it's signed: none are written here.

This release is between **Marchal Technologies LLC** ("the Company") and the person whose voice is
recorded ("the Speaker"). For a Speaker under 18, it is signed by the Speaker's parents on the Speaker's
behalf.

## 1. What's recorded

The Speaker records spoken lines and songs for **Ark Pals**, the Company's Bible adventure game for children,
in the game's recording studio: story pages, prompts, praise, learning lines, names, numbers, letters and
sounds, and (for the child) sung songs and short Pal lines ("the Recordings").

## 2. What the Company may do with them

The Speaker gives the Company a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to:
- play the Recordings **inside Ark Pals**, for every family that uses the game (the "house voices"), and
  store, copy and edit them (trimming silence, setting the level, joining a name onto a line) for that use;
- keep them on the Company's own servers and on the devices of families that play.

The license is **limited to Ark Pals**. The Company will not, without the Speaker's separate written
agreement:
- use the Recordings in advertising, trailers or other products;
- sell or license them to anyone else;
- use them to train or make a synthetic copy of the Speaker's voice.

## 3. The child's voice

For a Speaker under 18, the Recordings are **for the Company owner's own family only**, unless the owner
turns on sharing for a particular recording (a song or a Pal line). Each shared recording is chosen one at
a time, and can be unshared at any time. Her name is never written into the game.

## 4. Stopping

The Speaker (or, for a child, either parent; or the child herself once she turns 18) may ask in writing to
stop the use of any or all Recordings. The Company will remove them from the game within 30 days. Copies
already on families' devices stop being played at the next update.

## 5. No payment, no obligation

The Speaker records freely and is not paid. The Company doesn't have to use any Recording. The Speaker
keeps every other right in their own voice.
